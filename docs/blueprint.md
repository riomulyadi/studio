# **App Name**: TasteBook

## Core Features:

- Recipe Catalog Browsing: Browse a comprehensive catalog of recipes with options to filter by cuisine, meal type, or dietary restrictions.
- Detailed Recipe View: View complete recipe details including ingredients, step-by-step instructions, preparation time, and nutritional information.
- Recipe Submission: Allow authenticated users to contribute new recipes to the platform, including ingredients, steps, and optional images.
- AI Ingredient Swap Tool: An AI-powered tool that suggests ingredient substitutions or variations for a chosen recipe based on dietary needs or available pantry items.
- Personal Recipe Box: Users can save their favorite recipes to a personal collection for quick access and future cooking.
- Advanced Search & Filter: Utilize powerful search functionality to find recipes quickly by keywords, specific ingredients, or a combination of criteria.

## Style Guidelines:

- The overall scheme is light and warm, evoking a comforting and delicious culinary experience.
- Primary color: A deep, comforting reddish-brown (#A8441F), suggesting richness and earthiness.
- Background color: A very light, creamy off-white (#F7F2F0), providing a clean canvas that enhances warmth.
- Accent color: A soft, rosy-pink (#CC708B), used sparingly for interactive elements and highlights to add a touch of modern sophistication.
- Headline font: 'Belleza' (humanist sans-serif) for an elegant and art-inspired feel, suitable for captivating recipe titles.
- Body font: 'Alegreya' (humanist serif) for highly readable and engaging recipe instructions and longer descriptions.
- Utilize clean, line-based icons related to cooking, food, and meal preparation for clarity and ease of navigation.
- Use a grid-based layout for recipe listings, emphasizing imagery and clear, concise titles. Ensure generous white space to enhance readability.
